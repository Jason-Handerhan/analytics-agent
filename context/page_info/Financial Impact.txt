Executive Summary & Context

This interactive dashboard translates the machine learning primary evaluation metric (Recall @ 5) into corporate profitability. Utilizing the Instacart Kaggle dataset, I developed and evaluated classification and ranking models (XGBoost and LightGBM) to predict customer reorder patterns. By connecting the model scores to financial baselines derived from SEC filings, this tool allows the business to conduct sensitivity analysis (on conversion rate, churn rate, & AOV), illustrating how improvements in model recommendation performance could unlock tens of millions in savings.

How to Navigate This Page

Top Right Panel: Access global page toggle and supplementary information:

Data Split Toggle: Flip page metrics between Validation and Test datasets.

Donut Chart Icon: Opens a pop-up window displaying the profit lift share by lift driver (AOV, Conversion, Churn).

List Icon: Opens a pop-up window displaying assumptions used by the financial model.

Sensitivity Analysis Controls: Slide the inputs (Conversion Rate, Churn Rate, Conversion Lift, AOV Lift, Churn Reduction) to visualize financial impact under different business assumptions.

Projected Annual Profit by Model: Compares the baseline heuristic performance directly against every model candidate to visualize the total lift by driver.

Total Profit by Driver Waterfall: Tracks the sequential step-up from the baseline profit, due to each lift driver (AOV, Conversion, Churn).

Key Formulas & Definitions

Baseline Profit: Calculated directly from audited SEC filings as the starting financial anchor.

Baseline Profit = Annual Revenue ($3.742B) × Profit Margin (73.7%) ≈ $2.757B

Conversion Profit Lift: Captures incremental orders generated by recommending high-reorder items during an active shopping session.

Conversion Profit Lift = Extra Orders × Base AOV × Profit Margin

Where: Extra Orders = Annual Orders × (Recall Units × Conversion Lift per 10% Recall)

Churn Profit Lift: Measure the financial value of improving user retention due to relevant product recommendations.

Churn Profit Lift = Extra Customers Retained × Annual Orders/User × Base AOV × Profit Margin

Where: Extra Customers Retained = Active Customers × (Base Churn - New Churn)

AOV Profit Lift: Calculates the compound value of both new and existing transactions spending more per order due to effective cross-selling.

AOV Profit Lift = Extra Order AOV Lift + Extra Customer AOV Lift + Baseline Order AOV Lift
